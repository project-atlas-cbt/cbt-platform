# Student instructions

Read this aloud, or print it, before a sitting. It matches what students see on the screen before
they press Start.

1. **Sign in** at your school's address with the Student ID and password on your sign-in slip.
   The first time, you must choose a new password. Keep it to yourself.
2. Open **Assessment**. Your exam appears there when your teacher has opened it for your group.
3. Read the instructions on the screen. Wait until it says **This computer is ready for the
   exam**, then press **Start assessment**.
4. **Your time starts when you press Start** and is kept by the school's server, not this computer.
   The time left is always at the top of the screen.
5. Choose one answer for each question. Use **Next** and **Previous**, or the numbered buttons to
   jump to any question. You can change an answer until you submit.
6. Press **Submit assessment** when you are done, then confirm. If your time runs out, your exam
   is submitted for you.

**If the connection drops:** keep answering. Your answers are saved on this computer and sent when
the connection comes back; a message at the top says you are offline. **Do not reload
the page, close the browser or turn off the computer.** Tell your invigilator.

**Near the end of your time:** answers must reach the school's server within one minute after your
time runs out. Answers that arrive later, because the computer was disconnected for too long, are
not counted, and the screen will tell you which ones. If you see that the connection is down near
the end, tell your invigilator at once.

**If your computer stops working:** raise your hand. You will move to another computer, sign in
and press **Resume assessment**. Your answers and remaining time are kept.

**Keyboard:** `Tab` moves between controls and the arrow keys choose an answer. `Alt` + `N` goes
to the next question and `Alt` + `P` to the previous one.

**Results** appear under **Results** when your school publishes them.

Do not share your password, sign in for someone else, or try to open other websites during the
exam.
